# Waddle public launch checklist

Checked 2026-10-19 against the app and the linked primary-source guidance. This is a product checklist, not a published policy or legal advice. Do not submit a template policy to Google: its policy must describe Waddle's real practices and identify its operator.

Waddle's URLs are already public, and the booking page already collects guest names, required emails and optional notes. Publish accurate operator/contact and privacy information as soon as those details are settled; do not treat that work as merely a future launch step. The 2026-10-19 legal and privacy issue-spotting pass also identified the data and copy checks below.

## Ready in the app

- The account dialog and group gate already support Google sign-in. The same flow creates an account for a new user. Google Calendar is requested separately from basic sign-in, when the user chooses Connect in Calendar links.
- People can belong to multiple groups and have multiple friends. A friend's profile shows completed plans together after both friends RSVP Yes; earlier plans that were replaced before this history feature existed are not recoverable.
- A group invite can be opened as a draft text to a chosen phone number. On browsers with the Contact Picker API, the user can select one phone contact first. The selected number stays in the browser; Waddle does not upload the address book or send the text. Matching a person's whole phone address book to Waddle accounts is not implemented.
- A guest can join from an invite with just a name. The demo group works without an account.
- Settings have Auto, Light and Dark appearance plus nine accent colours, saved on the device.
- There is no advertising or client analytics tag in the app. Aggregate server metrics in `api/_metrics.js` have no user IDs or cookies. The app does use local storage for login sessions, preferences, calendar cache and group state, and a service worker for offline use. Third-party services include Supabase, Google sign-in/Calendar, Vercel and Google Fonts. Push notifications and booking emails are optional features.

## Before a public launch

1. **Get an operator identity and contact.** Decide whose name or organization runs Waddle, buy/choose a domain under that operator's control, and make a support/privacy email address. Use the same identity in the site, policy, Google consent screen and support replies. A dedicated Gmail address can be used while domain email is being set up. Decide intended regions, minimum age/children approach and service jurisdiction before finalizing the pages.
2. **Publish a Waddle-specific privacy policy on the verified domain.** It needs to say what account, group, friend, calendar, booking, push and device data is collected; what Google data is accessed; why and where each kind is stored; who receives it; how long it is retained; how to disconnect Google, delete the account/data, and contact the operator. Include non-account guests and booking visitors, pending friend requests to email/phone, cancelled bookings, group history/comments/photos, ICS URLs, Google tokens, push endpoints, notification logs, browser storage and backups. Name actual processors and any cross-border handling. Link the policy and contact from the public homepage, sign-in, guest join, booking page and app footer. Explain why booking email is required even before confirmation emails are enabled. Review the actual retention and deletion behavior before promising anything. Google requires a public HTML page and will not accept a sample/template policy.
3. **Publish terms appropriate to the chosen operator and jurisdiction.** Cover accounts, invitations and user content, permitted use, availability of a free service, responsibility for shared plans, ending service, and how to contact the operator. The operator must choose jurisdiction and any age rule before publication.
4. **Add account deletion and automatic expiry.** “Reset this device” only clears local data. Waddle needs self-service deletion of the Supabase account and associated data, with group management passed to another signed-in member. The operator chose automatic expiry rather than a separate removal form for unauthenticated guests and booking visitors; choose periods and enforce them before making a public promise. Define backup handling from [the data inventory](DATA_INVENTORY.md). Google disconnect now confirms server refresh-token deletion; profiles are now readable only by their owner and relevant friend-request parties.
5. **Decide the storage notice from the deployment's actual technologies and audience.** Document essential login/session and preference storage in the privacy/storage notice. If Waddle introduces nonessential analytics, advertising or other tracking, add the appropriate consent control before loading it. A generic banner that says “accept cookies” without corresponding controls would be misleading. UK rules cover local storage as well as HTTP cookies and provide limited exceptions for strictly necessary storage.
6. **Finish Google OAuth verification to remove the unverified-app warning for public users.** In Google Auth Platform, set the verified domain, app name, support email, homepage, privacy policy and (when available) terms URL. The homepage must be public and explain Waddle and the Google data request. Verify site ownership in Search Console using an account with access to the Cloud project. Check that requested scopes are the narrowest needed; Waddle currently requests `calendar.readonly` only on Calendar connection. Submit the app for brand and sensitive-scope verification with an end-to-end video showing consent and the Calendar feature. Test users are a temporary way to use an app in testing; they do not remove the warning for a public rollout.
7. **Resolve the disabled phone path.** The UI offers a texted code, but live Supabase Phone Auth has no SMS provider. Either configure and test one or hide the choice before public promotion.
8. **Run a real two-account check** using the production domain and real consent screens after verification. Keep development tests on fakes; do not send test emails or alter real calendars.
9. **Review link previews and hangout wording.** A live invite link can reveal the group name or plan title to a chat app's preview service; decide if this is acceptable or use generic previews for sensitive plans. A past plan counted after both friends RSVP Yes is an inferred hangout, not proof that they met. Explain that rule in the friend profile or help text.
10. **Check accessibility before wider promotion.** The booking day picker now uses ordinary buttons with a pressed state and preserves keyboard focus after selection. Continue testing keyboard access, focus handling, screen-reader labels and contrast for sign-in, dialogs, guest join and booking; fix observed failures.

## Possible next UI controls

- Compact or comfortable density for the week grid and cards.
- Week starts Sunday or Monday, separate from time-zone settings.
- Higher-contrast surfaces and larger text for accessibility.
- A simplified mobile home view focused on the next plan and RSVP.

These are proposals. Colour and appearance choices are already implemented.
